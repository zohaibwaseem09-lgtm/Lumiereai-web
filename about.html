About

We build the front desk that never clocks out.

Lumiere AI started with a simple observation: the businesses that are best at their trade are often the worst-equipped to answer their phone. A roofer mid-job can't take a call. A plumber under a sink isn't checking texts. Every one of those moments is a job at risk.

Why we started

Built by engineers, aimed at the trades

We're two engineering students who spent time building voice and messaging AI agents, and kept landing on the same conclusion: the highest-value place to apply this technology isn't another SaaS dashboard — it's the phone line of a business that's too busy doing the actual work to answer it. Roofers, plumbers, HVAC techs. People who make money with their hands, not by staring at a CRM.

How we work

Built, tested, then handed over

Every agent we ship is one we've built and tested on real call and message flows first. We don't hand you something theoretical — voice receptionist, SMS follow-up, and lead reactivation are all live systems, refined against real conversations before they ever reach a client's phone line.

What we believe

No call left unanswered

If a customer is ready to book, someone (or something) should be ready to answer — every time, not just during business hours.

Old leads aren't dead leads

A quote that didn't close last year is still a real person who once needed the work done. Most of them just need to be asked again.

Simple for the business owner

You shouldn't need to learn a new platform to benefit from this. The agents work quietly in the background of how you already run things.
