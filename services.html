Services

Three agents, built to work in sequence

Each one covers a different way a job slips through the cracks. Run one, or run all three as a single pipeline.

Stage 1 — Inbound

AI Voice Receptionist

A phone agent that answers every inbound call in your business's voice — books new appointments, reschedules existing ones, and handles cancellations, all without a customer ever hitting hold music. Built on Retell voice AI and connected to your calendar through n8n, so bookings sync automatically, no manual entry.

Handles

Booking, rescheduling, cancellations

Availability

24/7, including after hours and weekends

Stage 2 — Overflow

Missed-Call SMS Agent

Not every call gets picked up — you're mid-job, out of signal, or already on another line. The moment a call is missed, this agent sends an instant text to the caller and carries the conversation forward over SMS, right through to a booked appointment. It's the safety net behind the voice agent.

Triggers on

Any missed or unanswered call

Channel

SMS, two-way conversation

Stage 3 — Reactivation

Lead Reactivation Agent

Most businesses are sitting on months, sometimes years, of old quotes and contacts that never turned into jobs. This agent works back through that list automatically and re-opens the conversation — turning a static backlog into a live source of new bookings, without anyone manually dialing down a list.

Works from

Your existing lead / customer history

Goal

New bookings from contacts you already have
